Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to provide power or water?

Keegan brings the equipment, supplies. Please provide access to water & a power outlet, preferably an exterior one near where the vehicle will be parked.

How long does a detail take?

Most interior details take about 2-4 hours depending on the package, vehicle size and condition.

Do I need to be there?

Not necessarily. We just need access to the vehicle and the power outlet. Many clients stay home or work nearby.

What if the weather is bad?

Interior details can usually go ahead in light rain or cold weather. In severe weather we'll contact you to reschedule.

What is your cancellation policy?

Plans change. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule so we can offer the time slot to someone else. There's no fee.

How do I pay?

Payment is due after the service is completed. We accept Cash App, Venmo, PayPal and cash.

What areas do you serve?

All of Livingston County, Michigan.

How are add-ons priced?

Add-on estimates start at the listed minimum. The final price is confirmed after inspection.
